Fail-closed guarantees — verified by automated tests

Wrong recipient → BLOCKED before signature — funds moved: 0

Payment verifier binds payTo to the winning carrier account. A mismatched recipient is rejected before signature / settlement.

Tests: test/reservation-payment-verifier.test.ts, test/reservation-challenge-binding.test.ts, test/usdc-smoke-client.test.ts.

Duplicate retry → settle at most once (durable claim CAS)

Durable settle claim CAS ensures facilitator settle is invoked at most once per reservation attempt.

Tests: test/reservation-settle-claim.test.ts, test/reservation-adversarial.test.ts, test/final-demo.test.ts.

Failed settlement → no reservation, no ROUTE_RESERVED

ROUTE_RESERVED is published only after Mirror SUCCESS. Failed or inconclusive settlement cannot create an operational reservation.

Tests: test/reservation-service.test.ts, test/reservation-conclusive-failure.test.ts, test/phase6b-live-reservation.test.ts.

Live fact (not a negative live experiment): This live execution recorded exactly one facilitator settle call (settleCallCount: 1).
